## Live: hold-to-earn API credits

Hold $TCODE, link a Solana wallet by signature, and claim monthly Talocode Cloud credits (1 credit = $0.01 list). Credits are product usage, not cash. One wallet maps to one project. One claim per UTC month.

| Tier | Min $TCODE | Monthly credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorer | 1 | 1,000 |
| Builder | 100 | 10,000 |
| Ecosystem | 1,000 | 100,000 |
| Partner | 5,000 | 500,000 |

Claim at [dashboard.talocode.site](https://dashboard.talocode.site) or [Tera Blockchain Lab](https://teraai.chat/lab/blockchain). Public mint and tiers: `GET https://api.talocode.site/api/v1/cloud/tcode`.

## Not live

Contributor airdrops, a redemption floor, and staking-for-product-perks are planned. They are not available today.
